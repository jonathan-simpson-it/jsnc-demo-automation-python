# Term Sheet: Acme Corp Series A Financing

**Date:** January 15, 2026
**Status:** Non-Binding

## Parties

- **Company:** Acme Corp Limited (Hong Kong)
- **Investors:** Archbridge Capital Partners Fund I, LP (Lead)
- **Other Investors:** [To be determined]

## Key Terms

### Financing Terms
- **Round:** Series A
- **Pre-money Valuation:** HKD 390,000,000 (approximately USD 50,000,000)
- **Investment Amount:** HKD 78,000,000 (approximately USD 10,000,000)
- **Post-money Valuation:** HKD 468,000,000 (approximately USD 60,000,000)
- **Shares Issued:** 1,000,000 Series A Preferred Shares
- **Price per Share:** HKD 78.00

### Equity & Dilution
- **Founders (pre-round):** 70%
- **ESOP Pool:** 15%
- **Series A Investors:** 16.67%
- **Post-round Founder Ownership:** ~58.3%

### Liquidation Preference
- **Type:** 1x Non-participating Preferred
- **Conversion:** Automatically converts to Common upon IPO at minimum 2x initial purchase price

### Anti-Dilution Protection
- **Type:** Broad-based weighted average
- **Carve-outs:** Employee shares, strategic partnerships, equipment financing

### Governance
- **Board Composition:**
  - 2 Investor Directors (Archbridge + Co-investor)
  - 2 Founder Directors
  - 1 Independent Director (mutual agreement)
- **Observer Rights:** Lead investor has board observer rights

### Protective Provisions (Investor Veto Rights)
1. Amendment of charter documents
2. Creation of new equity classes
3. Debt exceeding $500,000
4. Acquisitions or mergers
5. Change in CEO or CFO
6. Dividends or distributions
7. Related party transactions exceeding $100,000

### Information Rights
- Monthly financial statements within 15 days
- Quarterly board packages within 30 days
- Annual audited financials within 90 days
- Annual budget by December 15
- Capitalization table updates

### Employee Matters
- **ESOP:** 15% fully diluted, refresh up to 5% annually
- **Key Person Insurance:** $5M on CEO and CTO

### Other Terms
- **Exclusivity:** 60 days from signing
- **Governing Law:** Hong Kong SAR
- **Disputes:** HKIAC arbitration
