# Investment Memo: Acme Corp — Series A

## Executive Summary

Acme Corp is a B2B SaaS company specializing in AI-powered contract analysis for financial institutions. Founded in 2023, the company has achieved $4M ARR with 85% gross margins and is seeking $10M in Series A funding.

## Company Overview

- **Founded:** 2023
- **Headquarters:** Hong Kong SAR
- **Employees:** 45 (Engineering: 20, Sales: 12, Operations: 8, G&A: 5)
- **ARR:** $4M (Q4 2025)
- **ARR Growth:** 120% YoY
- **Gross Margin:** 85%
- **Net Revenue Retention:** 130%

## Market Opportunity

The global contract management market is projected to reach $2.9B by 2027, growing at 12.5% CAGR. Financial institutions spend an average of $2.3M annually on manual contract review processes.

### Target Customers
- Tier 1 & 2 Banks
- Insurance Companies
- Asset Management Firms
- Private Equity Firms

## Product

Acme's AI platform automates contract review, risk assessment, and compliance checking. Key features include:

1. **Smart Contract Analysis:** NLP-powered extraction of key terms, obligations, and deadlines
2. **Risk Assessment Engine:** Real-time scoring of contract risks against regulatory requirements
3. **Compliance Module:** Automated checking against HKMA, SFC, and international standards

## Risk Factors

1. Customer concentration risk (top 3 clients = 40% of ARR)
2. Competition from well-funded US-based competitors
3. Regulatory changes in Hong Kong data privacy laws
4. Key person dependency on founding team

## Recommendation

**PROCEED** — Strong product-market fit, impressive growth metrics, and experienced founding team. The Hong Kong market position provides a defensible moat. Recommend $10M investment at $50M pre-money valuation.
